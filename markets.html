Where we buy, and where we sell

Material comes from suppliers abroad and goes to mills across South India. Both ends of that route are chosen deliberately.

Where we sell

Kraft and duplex mills across South India, most of them producing liner and medium for the packaging trade. We deal at management level and sell on repeat monthly programmes.

  • Tamil NaduKraft liner and medium producers
  • KeralaKraft and duplex board
  • KarnatakaKraft liner and packaging board
  • Andhra PradeshKraft and containerboard
  • TelanganaKraft liner and medium

Where we buy

We buy from recyclers, brokers and exporters wherever the grade and the freight work. Origin matters less to us than what is actually inside the bale.

  • EuropeDSOCC and DLK from sorting and box plants
  • North AmericaDSOCC #12 and #11 in container lots
  • Middle EastRegional collection and re-export
  • Southeast Asia and AustraliaShort-haul freight into Indian ports

Shipment and terms

Nothing exotic. Standard trade practice, agreed in writing before anything is booked.

  • PackingBaled and containerised, weights confirmed at load port
  • TermsCFR or CIF Indian ports, agreed contract by contract
  • DocumentsFull set as the contract requires, tracked from loading to arrival
  • ClaimsRaised on discharge evidence and settled directly between the parties

Volumes and shipment periods are agreed per contract. We would rather commit to a monthly programme we can hold than a tonnage we cannot.
